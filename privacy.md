# RVLS Rota Backup — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 October 2026**

## 1. Purpose

RVLS Rota Backup is a private application used to back up the automated duty rota system operated by Ribble Valley Live Steamers (RVLS).

## 2. Information processed

The application may process duty allocations, member names, configuration settings and operational records contained within the rota system's backup files.

The application uses Google OAuth authentication to obtain permission to access Google Drive.

## 3. Google Drive access

The application requests the `drive.file` permission to create, upload and manage its backup files in the authorised Google Drive account.

It does not require unrestricted access to the account's entire Google Drive.

## 4. Storage and security

Backup files are stored in a designated Google Drive folder accessible to authorised account holders.

Authentication credentials and access tokens are stored locally on the administrator's computer and are not published on this website.

## 5. Data sharing

The application does not sell personal information, use information for advertising or intentionally share rota data with third parties.

Google processes information as necessary to provide its Drive storage and authentication services.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Backup files are retained for operational recovery purposes and may be deleted by the authorised administrator when no longer required.

## 7. Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact the RVLS rota system administrator through Ribble Valley Live Steamers.
